# Rider–Waite–Smith card images

Artist: Pamela Colman Smith (1878–1951). Deck conceived by Arthur Edward Waite; first published by Rider & Company in 1909.

These are reproductions of the historical Pam-A printing, not images of a contemporary independently redrawn or recoloured commercial deck. The source repository identifies the historical images as public domain. Scan cleanup/restoration is credited to Steve P.

- Original scan collection: https://steve-p.org/cards/RWSa.html
- Download source and rights declaration: https://github.com/mixvlad/TarotCards/blob/main/SOURCES.md
- Source directory: https://github.com/mixvlad/TarotCards/tree/main/tarot/rider-waite/720px
- Independent historical-art reference: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RWS_Tarot_00_Fool.jpg (public-domain marking and original artist information; the local files were downloaded from the GitHub collection above, not from Commons).

Retrieved 6 November 2026. Each JPEG was verified against its Git blob SHA before conversion. `manifest.json` records the source filename and blob SHA, site card identifier, output dimensions and SHA-256 of each local WebP.

The site assets are resized proportionally to 480×800 and encoded as WebP at quality 82 for mobile delivery. No illustration is cropped, recoloured, redrawn or generated by AI. Cards may be rotated 180° in the interface to indicate reversed orientation; the source files remain upright.

The existing site-created card back is separate from this collection.
